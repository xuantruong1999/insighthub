InsightHub has been deployed to namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}!

Port-forward to access locally:
  kubectl port-forward svc/api {{ .Values.api.service.port }}:{{ .Values.api.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  kubectl port-forward svc/web {{ .Values.web.service.port }}:{{ .Values.web.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Health check:
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.api.service.port }}/healthz

Upload a document:
  curl -X POST http://localhost:{{ .Values.api.service.port }}/documents \
    -F "file=@sample-docs/so-tay-van-hanh.md"

Chat:
  curl -X POST http://localhost:{{ .Values.api.service.port }}/chat \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"question": "InsightHub co may thanh phan?"}'
